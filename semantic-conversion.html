What Is Semantic HTML?

Semantic HTML uses elements that describe the meaning and purpose of their content. Examples include header, nav, main, article, aside and footer.

Why Semantic HTML Matters

Semantic elements make webpages easier to understand, maintain and navigate. They also help assistive technologies identify important page regions.

Examples of Semantic Elements

  • header: Introductory content.
  • nav: Navigation links.
  • main: The main content of the page.
  • article: Self-contained content.
  • aside: Related or supplementary content.
  • footer: Closing or author information.

Learning Tip

Choose an HTML element based on the meaning of the content, not just how you want it to look.
